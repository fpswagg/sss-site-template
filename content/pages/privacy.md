When you write to us through the contact form, your name, phone or email and your message
are stored in our business tools (Sarena Shooting Star) so our team can answer you.

When you chat with our assistant, the conversation is kept so you can continue it later
from the same browser. You can start a new conversation at any time with **New chat**.

We never sell your information. To have it deleted, write to us from the [contact page](/contact).
